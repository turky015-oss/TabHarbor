# Store submission copy

## English

Name: TabHarbor

Short description: Save, organize, back up, and restore your tabs. Private, local storage with Arabic and English support.

Full description:

Your Tabs. Safe in One Place.

Keep your work, research, and ideas ready for later. TabHarbor saves your current window or all normal windows without closing your tabs. Restore entire sessions, individual pages, or a selected set whenever you need them.

Name sessions, mark favorites, search by page title or URL, and sort your collection. Automatic snapshots capture changes and keep a configurable history. Export and import JSON backups to move sessions between browsers or devices.

Choose English or Arabic with full right-to-left layout support, plus light, dark, and system themes. All session data stays in your browser. No accounts, ads, tracking, cloud services, or host permissions. Private browsing and internal browser pages are excluded.

Exported backups may contain private URLs. Keep them secure.

## Clipboard history update for 1.1.0

English addition: Optional clipboard history keeps text and images locally so you can copy them again later. Paste or upload manually, or enable automatic recording while the browser is running. Search, pin, preview and download saved items. Optional clipboard permissions are requested only when needed. Automatic recording can include sensitive information and copies from other apps/private windows; pause it before sensitive copies. It cannot recover content replaced before capture.
